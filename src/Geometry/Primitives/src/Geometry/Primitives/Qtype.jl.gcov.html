     384              :     QTypeSurface{T,D,M,N} <: ParametricSurface{T,D}
     385              : 
     386              : Surface incorporating the QType polynomials - radius and conic are defined relative to absolute semi-diameter, QType terms are normalized according to the `normradius` parameter.
     387              : `T` is the datatype, `D` is the dimensionality, `M` and `N` are the maximum QType terms used.
     388              : 
     389              : The surface is centered at the origin and treated as being the cap of an infinite cylinder, thus creating a true half-space.
     390              : Outside of 0 <= ρ <= 1 the height of the surface is not necessarily well defined, so NaN may be returned.
     391              : 
     392              : ```julia
     393              : QTypeSurface(semidiameter; radius = Inf, conic = 0.0, αcoeffs = nothing, βcoeffs = nothing, normradius = semidiameter)
     394              : ```
     395              : 
     396              : `αcoeffs` and `βcoeffs` should be a vector of tuples of the form `(m, n, v)` where `v` is the value of the coefficient ``α_n^m`` or ``β_n^m`` respectively.
     397              : 
     398              : The sag is defined by the equation
     399              : 
     400              : ```math
     401              : \\begin{aligned}
     402              : z(r,\\phi) = & \\frac{cr^2}{1 + \\sqrt{1 - (1+k)c^2r^2}} + \\frac{\\sqrt{1 + kc^2r^2}}{\\sqrt{1-(1+k)c^2r^2}} \\cdot \\\\

     404              : \\end{aligned}
     405              : ```
     406              : 
     407              : where ``\\rho = \\frac{r}{\\texttt{normradius}}``, ``c = \\frac{1}{\\texttt{radius}}``, ``k = \\texttt{conic}`` and ``Q_n^m`` is the QType polynomial index ``m``, ``n``.
